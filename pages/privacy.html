Privacy Policy

Last updated October 17, 2026

Who we are

Natarta (“Natarta”, “we” or “us”) operates the Natarta app. You can reach us at support.natarta@gmail.com.

The short version

What you check stays on your phone. Natarta has no accounts and no tracking, and we never sell your information.

What you check

Texts, emails, links, phone numbers, photos, videos and QR codes you check are looked at on your phone only. They are not uploaded to us or anyone else.

Your recent checks are saved on your phone so you can see them again. Deleting the app deletes them.

Scam alerts and your area (optional)

If you turn on local alerts, Natarta keeps only your general area (like a ZIP code or city and state) on your phone. If you choose “Use my approximate location”, your phone works out the area once and immediately discards the exact position. We never track where you go.

To show alerts, the app downloads public alert lists from our website (hosted by GitHub Pages). Like any website, GitHub may see basic technical details such as your internet address. The download does not include your area or anything you checked.

Speaking instead of typing (optional)

If you tap Speak, your phone turns your words into text. On most phones this happens on the phone itself. On some older phones, the phone’s built-in speech service (from Apple or Google) may process the audio under its own privacy policy. Natarta never receives or keeps the recording.

Safety list updates

About once a week the app downloads an updated list of known scam websites the same way. Nothing about you is sent.

The app also downloads, the same way, public headlines from trusted news outlets and fact-checkers, short “Happening now” scam reports based on official government alerts, and a list of phone numbers reported to the FCC (from the FCC’s public complaint data). Numbers you check are looked up on your phone and never sent.

If you tap “Check how old this site is”, only that website’s name (like example.com) is sent to rdap.org, a free public domain-registry service. Nothing else you checked is sent.

Tips and the Guardian badge

Tips are optional. Tapping “Support Natarta” opens a secure Stripe page in your browser. Stripe handles your payment details under its own privacy policy. We receive only what Stripe shares with merchants (like the amount and a receipt email if you give one), and we never see your full card number.

After a tip, the thank-you page can open Natarta to add a Guardian badge. The badge is saved only on your phone; we don’t link it to your payment or to you.

Links and sharing

When you tap a link (for example a fact-checker or an official warning), that website’s own privacy policy applies. When you share a result, you choose who gets it, and Natarta hides names, numbers, emails and codes in the shared card.

No ads, no selling, no tracking

Natarta has no ads, no advertising trackers and no analytics that follow you. We do not sell, rent or trade personal information.

Children

Natarta is made for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top and show it in the app. Questions? Email support.natarta@gmail.com.

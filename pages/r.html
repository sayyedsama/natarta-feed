      Good habits

        5 habits that stop most scams

        Pause. Real requests can wait a minute. Pressure to act now is the biggest sign of a scam.

        Verify another way. Contact them through their official app, website or a number you already have, not the one in the message.

        Never pay with gift cards or crypto. No real company, agency or bank asks to be paid that way. The same goes for wire transfers to strangers.

        Never share codes. A one-time code is a key to your account. Nobody honest will ask you to read it out.

        It's OK to hang up. You never owe anyone an instant answer. Hanging up or not replying is always allowed.

        Already replied or paid? Report it free at ReportFraud.ftc.gov Age 60+? Free help: 1-833-FRAUD-11
